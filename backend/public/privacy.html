2. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the Game, under contracts that limit their use of it: Google (Play Games sign-in, Play Billing, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging), Meta (Facebook Login, only if you choose it), Unity Technologies (Vivox voice chat), Supabase (database hosting) and Vercel (API hosting). We may disclose data if required by law.

3. Retention

We keep your account data while you use the Game. If you do not open the Game for 30 days — for example because you uninstalled it — your account and all its data are permanently deleted automatically. Linking Google or Facebook does not prevent this. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting laws require. Chat messages are deleted after 30 days. Crash and analytics data is kept according to Firebase's retention settings (maximum 14 months).

4. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the Game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This erases your sign-in links, friends, guild membership, chat messages, settings and push tokens immediately, and the rest of your account is permanently removed by our daily deletion job (within 24 hours). If you can no longer open the Game, use the account deletion request form; we process requests within 30 days.

5. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or the DPDP Act in India) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to certain processing. Email us to make a request.

7. Security

All traffic between the Game and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is restricted to our servers.

8. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the Game.
