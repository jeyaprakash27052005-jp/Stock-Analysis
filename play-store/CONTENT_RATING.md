## Target audience and content

**App content → Target audience:** select **13–15, 16–17 and 18+** only.
Do **not** select under-13 age groups: that puts the app under the Families policy (no Facebook login, restricted
analytics, teacher-approved rules). Answer "No" to "Could your app unintentionally appeal to children?" only if your
art style and store listing are clearly aimed at teens/adults.
