## Full description (≤ 4000)

```
Drop in. Loot up. Outlast the storm.

Stormdrop Royale is a fast, 10–15 minute battle royale built for phones. 50 players parachute onto a 4 km island,
scavenge weapons and gear, and fight to be the last one standing while a deadly storm shrinks the safe zone.
Survive to the end and earn the title of STORM CHAMPION!

GAME MODES
• Battle Royale – Solo, Duo or Squad (4 players)
• Clash Squad – quick 4v4 rounds, first team to 4 wins
• Team Deathmatch – respawn action, first to 30 eliminations
• Training Ground – practise every weapon offline
• Ranked – climb from Bronze to Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Heroic and Grandmaster

A LIVING ISLAND
Fight through Ironhaven City, raid Fort Basalt military base, hide in Goldmeadow Farms, hold the docks of Rustport,
snipe from Frostspine Peaks or cross the Serpent River. Day and night matches change every fight.

LOOT AND GUNPLAY
Assault rifles, SMGs, shotguns, snipers, LMGs, pistols and melee weapons. Customise them with 2x/4x/8x scopes,
suppressors, foregrips, magazines and stocks. Armor and helmets from level 1 to 4, backpacks, med kits, Vigor Shrooms,
grenades and deployable Foam Walls. Airdrops bring the rarest weapons – if you dare to grab them.

12 UNIQUE CHARACTERS
Every character has a signature skill: sprint bursts, healing auras, shields, dashes, scans and more.
Bring a pet companion for an extra edge.

SQUAD UP
Revive knocked teammates, ping enemies and loot, use quick chat or voice chat, join a guild and climb the leaderboards
with your friends.

DRIVE
Jeeps, motorbikes, buggies and boats – watch your fuel!

PLAYS GREAT ON ANY PHONE
Smooth, Standard and Ultra graphics. Runs on phones with 2 GB of RAM. Fully customisable controls with gyroscope aim
and auto-fire.

FAIR PLAY
Every shot is checked by our servers. Cheaters get banned.

Free to play. Optional in-app purchases for cosmetics, Storm Pass and Diamonds. Fortune Drop odds are always shown
before you spin. Internet connection required (except Training Ground).
```
